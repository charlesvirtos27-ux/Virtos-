Artistic approach

What I am searching for

My work is born from a tension between the visible and the intimate. I do not try to represent the world — I try to feel it on the canvas.

Each series begins with a different question. I work in cycles, through temporary obsessions — and every canvas is an attempt at a provisional answer.

"Painting is the way I found to say what I would not know how to put into words."
— Virtos Charles
